Sovereign: World Console — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · App ID com.purelink.sovereign · Developer: purelink

Sovereign: World Console ("the app") is a single-player strategy game. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

The short version

The app does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal or usage data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics in the current version.

What stays on your device

  • Game saves. Your progress is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Device backup. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's saved game in your device's own backup to your Google account. That backup is controlled by your device settings and Google's policies; the developer has no access to it.
  • Exporting a save. If you choose "Export", the app writes the save to a temporary file and opens your device's share sheet. The file goes only to the app or person you pick.

Network access

The app includes Android's standard internet permission because the framework it is built on adds it by default. The current version makes no network requests: the game and all of its world data are bundled inside the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds advertising, analytics, purchases, or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's Data safety section on Google Play will be updated to match. The effective date above will change whenever the policy does.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email the developer at the address shown on the app's Google Play page, or open an issue on this site's repository.
